### How to Install on Android

Since this app is distributed outside the Google Play Store, follow these simple steps to install:

1. **Download the APK**: Under the **Assets** section below, tap on `solat_malaysia_v1.2.8.apk` to download it.
2. **Open the File**: Once downloaded, open it from your browser notifications or your device's "Downloads" folder.
3. **Allow Unknown Sources**: If prompted, tap **Settings** and toggle on **"Allow from this source"** for your browser.
4. **Install**: Tap **Install**.
5. **Play Protect Prompt (Optional)**: If Google Play Protect shows a prompt regarding an unrecognized developer:
   * Tap on **"More details"** (or the small arrow pointing down).
   * Tap on **"Install anyway"**.
6. **Launch & Grant Permissions**: Open the app and allow Location and Notification permissions when prompted.
