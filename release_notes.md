## Solat Malaysia v1.8.0 — free prayer times app, no ads

A modern, futuristic prayer times app for Malaysia: official JAKIM times, Malay voice alerts, a real 3D Qibla globe, and a little robot that just wants to reach the mosque. **100% free. No ads. No sign-up. Open source.**

### ✨ What's new in v1.8.0
* **Autonomous-car countdown ring** — the Home countdown is now a road with a top-view self-driving car, a planned path ahead and an animated LiDAR sweep. **Swipe the car** and it spins around like a fidget spinner (faster swipe = more turns, faster sensors), then eases back to its real position.
* **Robot-to-mosque mini game** (Perihal tab) — a little robot drives to a modern mosque past random obstacles. Watch the preview run, then tap to make it jump; it hops funnily all the way into the mosque. Don't tap and it bonks, asks for help, reverses and retries… until the road disappears. Hehe!
* **Real 3D Qibla globe** — a rotating globe with a glowing route to Mecca. Pinch or tap zoom to swoop down to **satellite street level** (internet needed).
* **Malay male voice alerts & Bahasa Melayu by default** — "Telah masuk waktu Zohor…". English is one tap away in Settings.
* **Fresh look** — new minimalist app icon, dark dashboard design, all prayer times on one screen with no scrolling, and a dashboard-style home-screen widget.
* **Faster & more reliable** — quicker location, notification sound fixes, smoother animations (respects "remove animations").

### 📥 How to download & install

**Recommended — the guided download page (with screenshots and install steps):**
👉 **https://www.research.maizzat.my/downloads/solat_malaysia_download.html**

**Or directly from GitHub:** under **Assets** below, download `solat_malaysia_v1.8.0.apk` (about 57 MB, one universal APK for all modern Android devices, Android 8.0+).

1. **Download** the APK.
2. **Open** it from your browser notification or the *Downloads* folder.
3. If Android warns about unknown apps, tap **Settings** and enable **"Allow from this source"** for your browser.
4. Tap **Install**. (If Google Play Protect asks, tap *More details* → *Install anyway*.)
5. **Launch** the app and allow *Location* and *Notifications*.

### 🔄 Updating from an older version
Just install this APK over your existing app — your settings are kept. Apps that already include the update check show an **"Update Available"** pop-up on launch whenever a newer release is published here, and its **Update Now** button opens the download page above.
